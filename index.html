SETTINGS

LABELS
TRAILS
CURTAINS
GYRO

On a keyboard, L, T and C switch labels, trails and curtains too. The gyro turns the view as you turn the phone.

LOCK LINES

A line from a unit to everything in its LOCK/S list: red from a missile to the target it's flying at, white from a plane to the targets its pilot picked. Selected shows the lines of the unit you've picked, all shows everyone's.

MAP

UNITS

UI SIZE

Makes the panels and keys bigger or smaller. Handy on a phone.

CAMERA SLIDERS

Sliders on screen to zoom, turn and tilt the view. Handy on a phone. With the gyro on, the phone does the turning and tilting.

RANGE DOMES
SHOW FOR
SELECTED ONLY
GROUND WITHOUT RADAR

How far each unit's radar reaches, or how far it can see if it has no radar. Planes get a cone, as their radar only looks ahead. Ground units without a radar are hidden unless you turn them on. Selected only shows just the unit you've picked, whatever it is. In the game, hills, the curve of the earth and flying low cut it shorter, and big targets are seen further.
